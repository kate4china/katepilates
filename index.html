Meet Kate

Thoughtful movement, personal attention

Kate Pilates is built for people who want a quieter, more personal way to get strong. Each session is guided with clear cues, steady encouragement, and adjustments that make the work feel right for your body.

Whether you are new to Pilates, returning after time away, or refining an existing practice, Kate keeps the focus on progress you can actually feel: better alignment, easier posture, and strength that carries into daily life.
